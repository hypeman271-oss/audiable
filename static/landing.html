FAQ

The five questions writers ask.

Will it sound like a real audiobook?

Better than you'd expect. Not Audible-studio quality — we're honest about that. Play the samples above and judge for yourself; that's the whole point of putting them on the page.

Does my draft go to your servers?

Only if you turn on sync. Default is off — synthesis runs on your machine, the library lives in your browser, nothing leaves. Turn sync on and your draft + audio land on a Narrative server only YOUR devices can read. Never used as training data, never shared with other users. Flip sync off any time — your library stays on every device you've already synced.

Does it work with Scrivener, Word, or my Markdown notes?

Yes — import directly from Scrivener bundles, Word .docx, GitHub repos, Obsidian vaults, EPUB, and PDF. Or paste text. No file conversion needed.

Mac and Windows?

Yes, both. Linux on request. Android has a native app in closed beta. iPhone listening via the web app (add to home screen — works offline once installed).
